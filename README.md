### Hi, I'm Luiz 👋

A multiartist from Joinville, Brazil: frontend engineer by day, singer and composer by night. I design, prototype and code interfaces, and lately I've been obsessed with making them move.

- 🧠 Frontend Software Engineer at **GFT Technologies**, on the **Wynxx** team, building AI tools used by developers around the world
- 🎨 **6 years** of React, Next.js and TypeScript, plus product design from wireframe to production
- 🌀 Going deep on motion: **GSAP**, **Canvas 2D**, **WebGL** and **WebGPU**
- 🎶 Music is my other language, which is why some of my visualizers listen to it
